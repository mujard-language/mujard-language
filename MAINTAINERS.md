# Maintainers

## Current model: single maintainer (BDFL)

Mujard is maintained by one person today. The governance model, decision rights,
succession plan and the path to shared maintainership are defined in
`docs/governance/open-source/OSS-GOV-001` — read that document before assuming
committee processes exist; they do not yet.

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Maintainer / BDFL | Mujarhid Addillah (project author) | Final decision authority on language spec changes, releases, trademark, security priorities; repository write access |

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no application form — trust is earned through reviewed contributions
(`docs/governance/open-source/OSS-CON-003`). The maintainer may invite frequent
contributors to review privileges and, later, co-maintainer status. When the
project outgrows one maintainer, governance moves toward an elected council or
foundation per OSS-GOV-001 §4.3/§5.6.

## Contact

Security matters: `SECURITY.md` (private channel — never public issues).
Everything else: the issue tracker on this repository.
