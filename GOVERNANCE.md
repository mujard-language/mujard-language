# Governance

The open-source governance documents live in `docs/governance/open-source/` so they
stay versioned with the code. The platform's full policy register (internal,
standards and domain layers) is maintained in the platform repository, not here.

For the open-source components specifically:

- **Governance model** — `docs/governance/open-source/OSS-GOV-001-open-source-governance-policy.md`
  (maintainer/BDFL model, decision rights, succession plan)
- **Contributor process (DCO)** — `docs/governance/open-source/OSS-CON-003-contributor-and-dco-policy.md`
  and `CONTRIBUTING.md`
- **Language changes (RFC)** — `docs/governance/open-source/OSS-RFC-005-language-specification-governance.md`
- **Releases** — `docs/governance/open-source/OSS-REL-006-compiler-release-governance-policy.md`
  (the step-by-step checklist lives in the platform repository)
- **Security disclosure** — `SECURITY.md` and
  `docs/governance/open-source/OSS-SEC-008-open-source-security-disclosure-policy.md`
- **Supply chain / SBOM** — `docs/governance/open-source/OSS-SBOM-007-supply-chain-and-sbom-policy.md`
