# Trademark policy

The canonical policy is
`docs/governance/open-source/OSS-TM-004-trademark-and-branding-policy.md`.

Summary:

- "Mujard", the tagline *"Bare code. Every board."*, and the logo/app icons are
  project marks. The Apache-2.0 license grants **no** trademark rights.
- Permitted without permission: accurate compatibility descriptions, linking to
  the official project, academic/journalistic commentary, nominative use in forks
  that are clearly marked unofficial.
- Forks must rename or prominently mark themselves unofficial and must not use
  the logo or imply endorsement.
- Formal trademark clearance is still open — until registration is confirmed,
  treat the marks as claimed-but-unverified (OSS-TM-004 §1).
